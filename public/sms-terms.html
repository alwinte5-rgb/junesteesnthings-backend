Last updated: September 25, 2026

These terms cover text messages (SMS/MMS) sent by June's Tees & Things, a custom printing shop in Chicago, Illinois (jtees.net and design.jtees.net). By opting in, you agree to these terms.

1. The messages we send

There are two separate programs, and you choose each one yourself:

  • Order updates — messages about an order or quote you placed with us: quote ready, payment received, order status, proofs, pickup readiness, and shipping or delivery updates.
  • Deals & promotions — occasional offers, sales and new products. Only sent if you ticked the separate promotions box.
Agreeing to receive text messages is not a condition of any purchase. You can order from us without opting in to either program.

2. How you opt in

You opt in by ticking the text-message box(es) next to the phone number field when you request a quote, accept a quote, or check out on jtees.net or design.jtees.net. Both boxes are unchecked unless you tick them. We record the date, time and the wording you agreed to.

3. Message frequency

  • Order updates: frequency varies with your order, typically 1–5 messages per order.
  • Deals & promotions: up to 4 messages per month.

4. Cost

Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile plan. June's Tees & Things does not charge for text messages.

5. How to opt out

Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving texts from us. You will receive one final message confirming you have been unsubscribed, and no further messages after that. You can also email us at info@jtees.net or call (773) 849-1854 to opt out. To rejoin, reply START.

6. Getting help

Reply HELP to any message for help, or contact us at info@jtees.net or (773) 849-1854.
