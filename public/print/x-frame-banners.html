The product

X-frame banners: a sign that stands up anywhere

X-frame banners are the simplest standing sign there is. A light X-shaped frame holds a printed banner tight at its four corners, so it stands on its own beside a booth, at a lobby door, at the front of a church or at a check-in table, with no wall, no stand to rent and no tools. We print custom X-frame banners 23.6 inches wide by 63 inches tall on matte vinyl, which shows color well without glare under event lights, and sell them with the frame or as a banner alone.

Banner only, or banner and stand

Order the banner with its X-frame stand for your first one. If you already own a frame of the same size, order the banner only and swap designs for each event or season: a welcome sign for Sunday, a sale for the spring market, a sponsor banner for the tournament. The price table above lists both, for one banner and for larger orders.

Where X-frame banners work best

Vendor booths and pop-up shops, trade shows and career fairs, restaurant and salon entrances, church lobbies and welcome tables, school open houses, sports registration, real estate open houses, grand openings and photo spots at parties. Because the banner is tall and narrow, it is read at eye level from across a room, even when a crowd is standing in front of the table.

Designing a tall banner

Put your logo and name at the top, where people see it over a crowd, and keep the message short: a few words, a phone number or website and a QR code if you want people to scan. Leave the bottom area simple, since it sits behind tables and people. We check every file before it prints, and we can design the banner for you and send a proof. Design is a separate fee shown on your quote, and nothing prints until you approve the proof.

Matching your table and your team

Pair an X-frame banner with a printed table cover and matching shirts for your team, and the whole booth looks like one brand. We can design and quote all of it together. Tick each item on the quote form below and tell us about your event and its date.

Setting up and caring for your banner

An X-frame stand folds into a short bundle that fits in a car trunk or a closet. To set it up, open the frame, hook the four corners of the banner onto the arms and stand it up; it takes about a minute and needs no tools. Roll the banner with the printed side facing out when you take it down, and keep it dry and away from heat between events so it lies flat the next time. With a little care, one banner and stand are used at event after event, which makes it one of the best value signs a small business or group can own.
