The product

Custom stickers and labels for your brand

Custom stickers and labels put your name on everything you sell and give away. A logo sticker on a bag, a box or a laptop is advertising that travels, and a clean product label makes a homemade candle, hot sauce or soap look ready for a store shelf. We print two kinds: cut-to-shape stickers on white permanent vinyl, made one by one for handing out, and gloss laminated roll labels on a roll, made for putting on products quickly and neatly.

Cut-to-shape stickers

Our stickers are printed in full color on white vinyl with a permanent adhesive and cut to shape. They come in 2 inch and 3 inch circles and 2 by 3 inch and 3 by 4 inch rectangles, from 10 stickers. They are made for handing out at events, tucking into orders, sealing bags and boxes, decorating water bottles and laptops, and for team, school and church giveaways.

Roll labels for products and packaging

Roll labels are printed on white gloss BOPP, a plastic film that holds up to moisture and handling better than paper, with a gloss laminate on top. They come on a roll so you can peel and apply them fast, by hand or with a label dispenser, in the same circle and rectangle sizes as the stickers, from 25 labels. They suit jars, bottles, candles, cosmetics, coffee bags, boxes and mailers.

Designing a label that works

Keep text large enough to read at arm's length, leave a little space between the text and the edge, and use your brand colors. For a product label, include what it is, your brand, the size or weight and your website. If your label needs ingredients, warnings or other required wording, you are responsible for what it says; we print it exactly as approved. We check every file before printing, and we can design stickers or labels for you for a separate design fee shown on your quote.

Stickers, labels and the rest of your order

Many customers order stickers or labels alongside shirts, tote bags, pouches or table covers, so a market booth or a product launch has one look from the table to the bag. Tick everything you need on the quote form below, with a quantity for each, and we will quote it all together on one written quote.

Choosing a size and a shape

A 2 inch circle suits a logo on a bag, a box seal or a small jar lid. A 3 inch circle gives a logo room for a short line under it, such as your website or a tagline. A 2 by 3 inch rectangle is a good size for a simple product label with a name and size, and a 3 by 4 inch rectangle fits a full product label with a description, ingredients or directions. If you are not sure, measure the flat area of the item the label goes on and tell us on the quote form, and we will recommend the size that fits it best before anything is printed.

How ordering works

Choose a size and a quantity, tell us whether you want stickers or roll labels, and send your artwork or ask for design. We reply with a written quote, usually within one business day. Once the quote is paid and the proof approved, your order goes to print and is ready at our shop in the number of business days shown above the price table. Your order is delivered to our Lakeview shop. Pick it up by appointment, or ask about local delivery or shipping, which we quote with your order.

Common questions

Custom Stickers & Labels FAQs

What is the difference between stickers and roll labels?
Stickers are cut one by one on white vinyl, for handing out and decorating. Roll labels come on a roll, laminated, for putting on products and packaging quickly.
What sizes do you offer?
2 inch and 3 inch circles, and 2 by 3 inch and 3 by 4 inch rectangles, for both stickers and roll labels.
What is the smallest order?
10 stickers or 25 roll labels. Larger orders cost less each, as the price tables above show.
Are the labels waterproof?
Roll labels are printed on a plastic film with a gloss laminate, which stands up to moisture and handling far better than paper. Tell us how your product is stored and we will advise.
Can you design my label?
Yes. Send your logo and the wording and we design it and send a proof. Design is a separate fee shown on your quote.
Which size should I choose?
Measure the flat area where the sticker or label will go and tell us on the quote form. We recommend the size that fits before anything is printed.
Do the prices include tax and shipping?
Every price includes shipping to our Lakeview shop. Sales tax is added to your quote, and delivery or shipping to you is quoted separately if you need it.
