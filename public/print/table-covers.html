The product

Custom table covers that make a booth look finished

Custom table covers turn a plain folding table into a branded booth in a minute. At a vendor market, a trade show, a church fair or a school night, a printed cover tells people who you are from across the room and hides boxes and stock under the table. We print custom table covers for standard 6 foot and 8 foot tables, with your logo, colors, name and message printed all over the outside of the cover, starting with a single cover.

Open back or closed back

A 3-sided cover is open at the back, so you can sit behind the table and reach supplies underneath without lifting the cover. A 4-sided cover closes all the way around, which looks neat from every side for a table people can walk around, or a table at the front of a room. Both are printed all over the outside, not just with a logo patch on the front, so the whole cover carries your design.

Who uses printed table covers

Small businesses selling at markets and pop-ups, food vendors, artists, authors at book signings, nonprofits at resource fairs, churches at community days, schools and sports teams at registration nights, recruiters at career fairs and companies at trade shows. A table cover is reused at every event, so one cover pays for itself many times over and looks the same at the tenth event as at the first.

Getting the design right

A table cover is seen from a few steps away, so big, simple designs work best: your logo, your name, one short line and your website or social handle. We check your artwork before anything is printed, and if you would like us to design it, we lay out the front, sides and top for you and send a proof. Design is a separate fee, shown on your quote before work starts. Nothing is printed until you approve the proof.

Pairing a cover with a banner

Many booths pair a table cover with an X-frame banner standing beside the table, so people can read your name above the crowd as well as on the table. Both can be designed together so the colors and logo match, and quoted together on one quote. Just tick both on the quote form below and tell us about your event.

Artwork that reads from across the room

The best table cover designs are bold and simple. A large logo in the center of the front panel, your business name in big letters and one line about what you do will be read by people walking past, where small print and long lists are lost. Use high contrast colors, such as dark text on a light background or white text on a dark one, and send your logo as a vector file or a large, sharp image. If all you have is a small logo, we can usually redraw it cleanly as part of the design, and we will tell you before the proof if anything needs attention.

How ordering works

Choose your table size and style, tell us how many covers you need and when your event is, and we reply with a written quote, usually within one business day. Once the quote is paid and the proof approved, your covers go to print and are ready at our shop in the number of business days shown above the price table. Your order is delivered to our Lakeview shop. Pick it up by appointment, or ask about local delivery or shipping, which we quote with your order.

Common questions

Custom Table Covers FAQs

Which size do I need?
Measure the length of your table. Standard folding tables are 6 feet or 8 feet long, and we print covers to fit both. If you are not sure, tell us the table's length on the quote form.
What is the difference between 3-sided and 4-sided?
A 3-sided cover is open at the back so you can sit behind the table and reach under it. A 4-sided cover goes all the way around for a table seen from every side.
Is the whole cover printed?
Yes. The whole outside of the cover is printed with your design, not just a logo on the front.
Can I order just one?
Yes, from one cover. The price each comes down as the order grows, as the table above shows.
Can you design it for me?
Yes. Send your logo and what you want it to say and we design it and send a proof. Design is a separate fee shown on your quote.
What file should I send for my logo?
A vector file such as AI, EPS, SVG or PDF is best. A large PNG or JPG can work too, and we tell you before printing if it is too small to print sharply at table size.
Do the prices include tax and shipping?
Every price includes shipping to our Lakeview shop. Sales tax is added to your quote, and delivery or shipping to you is quoted separately if you need it.
