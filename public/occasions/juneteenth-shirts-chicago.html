Ideas & planning

Juneteenth shirt designs

Juneteenth shirts celebrate June 19, 1865, the day freedom reached Galveston, and the holiday we celebrate today. Our customers' favorite designs say "Juneteenth Is My Independence Day", "Since 1865" or "Free-ish Since 1865", carry the definition of the word, or use a portrait in red, gold and green. As a Black-owned shop, these designs mean something to us, and we love helping families and groups make their own.

Family, festival and church Juneteenth shirts

Family reunions and cookouts on Juneteenth weekend order matching shirts with the family name and the year; festivals, block parties and community organizations order event shirts with sponsors on the back; churches order shirts for Juneteenth services and celebrations. We print every size from toddler to 5XL.

Shirts for Greek organizations

Fraternities and sororities order Juneteenth shirts in their colors with their letters, for service projects, parades and step shows. We match colors closely and can add chapter names and line numbers.

Juneteenth festival and community event shirts

Block clubs, park districts, nonprofits and businesses hosting Juneteenth festivals order event shirts with the name, the date and sponsor logos on the back, plus volunteer shirts in a second color so helpers are easy to spot. We also print the banners, yard signs and step-and-repeat backdrops for the stage and the photo area.

Kids' Juneteenth shirts

Kids' Juneteenth shirts with bright colors and encouraging words ("Proud", "Free", "Brilliant") are perfect for school celebrations, camps and family cookouts. We print youth and toddler sizes and onesies in the same designs as the adults, so the whole family can match.

The meaning behind the colors

Many Juneteenth designs use red, black and green from the Pan-African flag, or the red, white and blue of the Juneteenth flag with its star and burst, created in 1997 to represent a new freedom for all African Americans. We are happy to help you choose a palette that fits your message and your event.

Business and employee Juneteenth shirts

Companies and employee resource groups order Juneteenth shirts for volunteer days, heritage events and parade groups. We match your company colors, add your logo on the sleeve, and sort orders by team or location.

Designs we can create for you

Our designer creates original Juneteenth artwork: portraits, typography, family crests, and designs that include your family name or your organization. Send us your ideas and we send a proof before printing.

How to order Juneteenth shirts

Send us your design idea or choose one of ours, the colors, the sizes and your date. We send a proof you can change, then print in our Lakeview shop. Pick up by appointment or we ship anywhere in the US, which is handy for families traveling to a reunion in another state.

Juneteenth family reunion shirts

Juneteenth weekend is a favorite time for family reunions and cookouts. Reunion shirts that pair the family name with "Juneteenth" and the year, or a family tree in red, black and green, celebrate both freedom and family. We print every size from toddler to 5XL and can list each branch of the family on the back, so everyone sees their name.

Many families keep the design and order again each year with the new year on the front.

When to order Juneteenth shirts

Order in May for the best timing; our standard turnaround is 7 to 14 business days after proof approval. Pick up at our Lakeview shop by appointment, or we ship anywhere in the US.

When to order: Juneteenth is June 19. Order at least 4 weeks ahead: standard turnaround is 7 to 14 business days after you approve the proof.
