Ideas & planning

Black History Month shirt ideas

Black History Month shirts celebrate pride, history and excellence. Popular designs carry words like "Talented, Kreative, Black", a map of Africa in red, gold and green, portraits, "Black King" and "Black Queen" designs, or the names of the leaders and inventors a school is studying. As a Black-owned shop, we design many of these ourselves and love creating something new with you.

Shirts for schools, churches and organizations

Schools order Black History Month shirts for every class and staff member, often with a theme for the month; churches order shirts for Black History programs; companies and employee groups order shirts for heritage events. We sort and bag by class or group, and bulk orders get price breaks at 12, 24, 48 and 100 or more.

Custom artwork and banners

Need a design that is yours alone? Our graphic designer creates custom artwork for programs, events and fundraisers, and we print it on shirts, hoodies, banners and posters so everything matches.

Classroom and school-wide shirts

Many schools choose one design for the whole school with each class in its own color, or let each class design a shirt around a leader they studied. We print youth and adult sizes, sort and bag by class, and can add the school name and year. Teachers often order a staff shirt in a matching design.

Church Black History programs

Churches order shirts for Black History Month programs, youth presentations, choir performances and Sunday services, along with banners and programs for the event. A theme verse, the church name and the year make the shirts a keepsake for the congregation.

Companies and employee groups

Employee resource groups and companies order Black History Month shirts for heritage events, volunteer days and lunch-and-learns. We match company colors, place the logo where it fits, and can print each team's name on the sleeve.

Empowering words and designs

Words like "Excellence", "Legacy", "Unapologetically Black", "Melanin", "Black History Is American History" and "Dream Big" are popular, as are designs that honor inventors, authors, athletes and civil rights leaders. Our designer can build a design around your theme, your school or your family.

Year-round pride and heritage apparel

Many customers order heritage designs to wear all year, not just in February: family reunion shirts with African prints, Juneteenth shirts, and shirts celebrating HBCUs and Greek organizations. We are proud to be a Black-owned, women-owned Chicago print shop.

How to order

Send the theme, the design idea or the leaders you want to honor, the sizes and your date. Our designer sends a proof you can change, and we print in our Lakeview shop. Schools and churches can pay by invoice, and we sort and bag orders by class or group so handing them out takes minutes. Pick up by appointment or we ship anywhere in the US.

Posters, banners and program materials

Black History Month programs often need more than shirts: posters of the leaders a class is studying, a banner for the stage, programs for the audience, and photo backdrops for families. Our graphic designer can create a matching set from one theme, and we print the banners, posters and shirts together so the program looks unified from the first slide to the final bow.

When to order

Order in early January for February events; our standard turnaround is 7 to 14 business days after proof approval. Pick up at our Lakeview shop by appointment, or we ship.

When to order: Black History Month runs through February. Order at least 2 weeks ahead: small orders take 5–7 business days and larger orders 7–14 after you approve the proof. Need it sooner? Rush is available for a fee.
