Ideas & planning

Witch squad and group Halloween shirts

Custom Halloween shirts are the easiest group costume there is. Witch squad sets give everyone their own witch (Good Witch, Bossy Witch, Quiet Witch, Crazy Witch, Little Witch), ghost designs like "I'm Just Here for the Boos" and "Boo Bees" are made for parties, and skeleton hands crop tops are a favorite for nights out. Send us the list of names or titles and we print one for each person.

Family and kids Halloween shirts

Families order matching shirts for trick-or-treating, school parades and fall festivals, with a pumpkin, a ghost or a funny line for each person. We print youth and toddler sizes and onesies, and glow-style and metallic inks for something extra.

Halloween party and event shirts

Bars, haunted houses, trunk-or-treats and office parties order staff and event shirts; teams order Halloween practice shirts. Bulk orders get price breaks at 12, 24, 48 and 100 or more.

Halloween shirt ideas for adults

Adults love Halloween shirts with a wink: "I'm Just Here for the Boos", "Stop Staring at My Boos", "Resting Witch Face", "Hocus Pocus I Need Coffee to Focus", or a skeleton hands crop top for a night out. Group sets with a different witch, ghost or monster for each friend make the easiest costume at any party.

Couples and family costume shirts

Couples order matching pairs ("He's My Treat" and "She's My Trick", skeleton hands, a pumpkin and a seed), and families order a theme with a role for every person, like a witch coven, a ghost family or a haunted house crew. We print every size from onesies to 5XL so even the baby can match.

Trunk-or-treat and fall festival shirts

Schools, churches and park districts running trunk-or-treats, fall festivals and haunted houses order volunteer and staff shirts with the event name and date, plus banners and yard signs to point families in the right direction. Bulk pricing starts at 12 pieces.

Fabrics and inks for Halloween

Black, orange, charcoal and purple shirts are the Halloween favorites, and white or glow-style inks make designs pop at night. We print on tees, hoodies, crewnecks and crop tops, and on totes and pillowcase-style bags for trick-or-treating.

Why order Halloween shirts early

October is one of our busiest months for group orders. Ordering by late September gives you time to collect names and sizes, approve your proof, and still pick up with time to spare before parties start the weekend before Halloween.

How to order Halloween shirts

Pick a design or send us your idea, then send the names or titles for each person and their sizes. We send a proof you can change and print in our Lakeview shop. Pick up by appointment or we ship. For groups, one person can collect everyone's choice and we bag each shirt with a name so handing them out at the party is easy.

Halloween shirts for businesses

Bars, restaurants, salons and boutiques order Halloween staff shirts to get into the spirit, and haunted houses and escape rooms order staff and scare-actor shirts in black with glow-style prints. Businesses also order Halloween window decals, yard signs and banners to bring in customers through October. Ask us for a quote on the whole set.

When to order Halloween shirts

Order by late September or the first week of October; our standard turnaround is 7 to 14 business days after proof approval. Pick up at our Lakeview shop by appointment, or we ship.

When to order: Halloween is October 31. Order at least 4 weeks ahead: standard turnaround is 7 to 14 business days after you approve the proof.
