Ideas & planning

Personalized Valentine's gifts people keep

Custom Valentine's Day gifts feel personal because they are: a wine glass with "Be Mine" and their name, a wine bag and glass set wrapped with roses, a wooden wine gift box with an inside joke on the front, or a photo panel of the two of you. We make each one to order in our Lakeview, Chicago shop, so it says exactly what you want it to say.

Gift sets for Galentine's Day, for the whole friend group or for coworkers, are just as popular: matching glasses or tumblers with each name, ready to hand out.

Couples paint-and-sip kits

Our couples paint kits come with pre-drawn canvases that fit together (King and Queen, a lock and key, roses and a toast), brushes, paint and matching glasses, so date night at home becomes a painting party. They make a gift and an activity in one, and we can personalize the canvases with names and a date.

Matching couples and Galentine's shirts

Matching couples shirts ("Mr. and Mrs.", "His and Hers", or a design with your anniversary date), lips and heart designs, and Galentine's crew shirts for a girls' night out are all printed with no minimum. Add a photo, your names or a date, in red, pink, black or white.

Valentine's gifts for him

For him, engraved flasks and stainless tumblers with his initials, a hoodie with a private joke or your photo, a couples paint kit for a night in, or a wine and glass gift set with his name. Matching "His" and "Hers" shirts for a Valentine's trip are always popular.

Valentine's gifts for her

For her, a wine glass gift set with roses and her name, a glitter "Wifey" or "Bae" shirt, a photo panel or canvas of a favorite picture, a tumbler with her name, or a Galentine's set to share with her friends. Every piece is made to order, so it can say exactly what you would say.

Valentine's for classrooms and businesses

Teachers order Valentine treat bags and shirts for classroom parties, and salons, restaurants and boutiques order Valentine's staff shirts, gift card sleeves and window decals. Small businesses use custom wine glasses and gift boxes as Valentine's promotions and raffle prizes.

Ideas for what to write

Short lines work best: "Be Mine", "Cupid's Favorite", "Mine Forever", your names with a heart between them, the date you met, or a nickname only the two of you understand. On wine glasses and flutes, a first name and a small heart look elegant; on shirts, bold lettering and a photo make a statement.

How to order a Valentine's gift

Tell us who the gift is for, what you want it to say, and the date you need it. We suggest a design, send a proof you can change as many times as you need, then make it in our Lakeview shop. Gift sets can be wrapped and ready to hand over, and you can pick up by appointment or have it shipped to you or straight to them. Ordering early leaves room to add a second gift, like matching glasses for a Galentine's brunch.

Valentine's Day in Chicago

From a dinner in Lincoln Park to a night in, a personalized gift makes the evening. Chicago customers pick up by appointment at our Lakeview shop, a short ride from Lincoln Park, North Center, Roscoe Village and Logan Square, so a last look at the finished gift is easy. Gifts can also ship anywhere in the US for long-distance couples.

When to order Valentine's gifts

Order by the end of January for the best timing; our standard turnaround is 7 to 14 business days after proof approval, and February 14 arrives quickly. Pick up at our Lakeview shop by appointment, or we ship.

When to order: Valentine's Day is February 14. Order at least 4 weeks ahead: standard turnaround is 7 to 14 business days after you approve the proof.

Common questions

Custom Valentine's Day Gifts FAQs

When should I order Valentine's Day gifts?
By the end of January. Turnaround is 7 to 14 business days after you approve the proof.
Can you put names on wine glasses?
Yes, names, dates and short messages on wine glasses, flutes, shot glasses and tumblers.
What comes in a couples paint kit?
Pre-drawn canvases, paint, brushes and a palette, with matching glasses when you choose the gift set.
Can I order just one gift?
Yes. There is no minimum on personalized gifts.
Do you make Galentine's Day gifts for a group?
Yes, matching glasses, tumblers or shirts with each friend's name.
Can I add a photo?
Yes, on shirts, photo panels and many gifts. Send the clearest photo you have.
Do you make Valentine's gifts for men?
Yes: engraved flasks, tumblers, hoodies, shirts and gift sets.
Can you make classroom Valentine's shirts?
Yes, for teachers and whole classes, in youth sizes.
Can I get a gift box with a message?
Yes, a wooden wine gift box printed with your message.
Can you ship the gift directly to them?
Yes, anywhere in the US, with your message inside.
Can I see the design first?
Yes, every order gets a proof before it is made.
