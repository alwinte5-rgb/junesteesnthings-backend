Custom Church Group Shirts in Chicago — Ministry & Event Ideas

📅 July 2026 🕒 6 min read ✍️ June's Tees & Things

From Sunday service to the summer revival, matching shirts unite the congregation. Here's how to design ministry, choir, usher board, youth group, and church event shirts in Chicago — with bulk pricing and no minimums.

Shirts for Every Ministry

Give each ministry its own look — the choir, the usher board, the deacons, the praise team, the men's and women's ministries, and the children's church. Add your church name, a scripture, and your logo, and we'll keep the design consistent across every group and size.

VBS, Revivals, Anniversaries & Conferences

Big church events call for big orders, and that's where we shine. Vacation Bible School, church anniversaries, revivals, women's conferences, mission trips, and fellowship days — we print them all, and the per-shirt price drops as the count goes up.

Youth Group & Fellowship Tees

The youth group loves matching shirts for lock-ins, retreats, and community service days. Bright, bold designs with a fun scripture or theme print beautifully and build unity — design them online or let us help.

Design It Online, or Let Us Help

Open our Custom Church Group Shirts collection in the Design Studio, add your church name, scripture, and colors, and our AI design help builds a starting point in seconds. Prefer a hand? Send us the details and we'll design it for you.

Bulk Pricing, No Minimums

Order shirts for the whole congregation or just one ministry — there are no minimums, and volume discounts kick in as your order grows. Mix any sizes from children's church to the senior ministry at the same design price.

Ready to Outfit Your Church?

Start in the Church Design Studio, or tell us what you need and we'll take it from there — bulk pricing, fast Chicago turnaround, from a Black-owned, women-owned local shop. Call or text (773) 900-4592.
