Birthday & Kids Shirts Made Easy

📅 July 2026 🕒 5 min read ✍️ June's Tees & Things

Add names, ages, and themes — perfect matching shirts for the birthday star and the whole crew, printed with no minimums in Chicago.

Start With the Birthday Star

The guest of honor's shirt does the heavy lifting: their name, their age, their favorite colors or theme. Photo shirts are a favorite — bring us a picture and we'll print it crisp and full-color, sized right for the shirt.

Matching Crew Shirts Without the Headache

“Birthday Crew,” “Squad of the Birthday Girl,” mom and dad editions — we keep the design consistent across every size from toddler tees to adult 5XL, so the group photo looks right.

Kids' Themes That Print Beautifully

Numbers with crowns, sports themes, glitter vinyl names — bold and simple prints read best on small shirts. We'll help you adapt any theme so it prints clean and lasts through the wash.

No Minimums, Real Proofs

Order one keepsake shirt or twenty for the party. Most orders take 7–14 business days, so tell us your party date and order a few weeks ahead.
