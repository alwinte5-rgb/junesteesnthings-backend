Ravenswood mixes families, makers, and small business — and we print for all of them. Common Questions

Custom T-Shirts Chicago FAQs

Where can I get custom t-shirts near me in Chicago?
Right here — June's Tees & Things is a custom print shop at 3047 N Lincoln Ave #435 in Chicago's Lakeview neighborhood, serving Lincoln Park, North Center, Logan Square, and all of Chicagoland. Design online at design.jtees.net or call (773) 849-1854, and pick up curbside or have it shipped.
Is there a minimum order?
No minimums. Order a single custom shirt or hundreds — DTF printing makes one-off shirts affordable, and larger orders get volume discounts.
How fast can I get custom shirts?
Most orders turn around in about 7 days, and many are faster. Have a deadline — an event, party, or game? Tell us the date and we'll confirm we can hit it.
Can I design my own shirt online?
Yes. Our online Design Studio lets you upload art, add text and graphics, or use built-in AI design help — see it on the shirt and get live pricing before you order. Prefer help? Send us the idea and we'll design it for you.
Which print method should I choose?
For 12+ shirts, screen printing is the best value. For full-color designs, photos, or small quantities, DTF transfers have no minimums. For polos, hats, and a premium look, choose embroidery. Not sure? We'll recommend the right one.
Do you offer curbside pickup?
Yes — curbside pickup is available Monday–Friday, 10:30 AM–6 PM at our Lincoln Avenue shop. Call or text (773) 849-1854 when you arrive.
