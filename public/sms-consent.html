How Customers Opt In to Text Messages

Last updated: October 3, 2026

June's Tees & Things, a custom printing shop in Chicago, Illinois, only texts people who have opted in on our website. This page shows exactly what a customer sees when they do.

Where customers opt in

Next to the phone number field on our quote request form at www.jtees.net, and at checkout in our online design studio at design.jtees.net. The same two checkboxes appear in both places.

The exact wording

There are two separate choices. Each box is unchecked until the customer ticks it, and either can be ticked without the other.

Box 1 — order updates:

Text me about my order (quotes, order status, pickup and delivery updates) from June's Tees & Things. Msg frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help.

Box 2 — deals and promotions:

Also text me deals and promotions from June's Tees & Things (up to 4 msgs/month). Consent is not a condition of purchase. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out.

Directly below the boxes: "See our SMS Terms and Privacy Policy."

Promotional texts go only to customers who ticked the second box. Agreeing to texts is not a condition of any purchase. We record the date, time and exact wording each customer agreed to.

Opting out and getting help

Reply STOP to any message to stop all texts, START to rejoin, or HELP for help. You can also reach us at info@jtees.net or (773) 849-1854.
