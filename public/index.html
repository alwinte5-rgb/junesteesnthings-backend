Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you have minimums?
No minimums! Order a single shirt or hundreds — DTF printing lets us do one-offs at a great price, and bulk orders get volume discounts.
How much does a custom shirt cost?
Pricing depends on the product, the number of ink colors, and how many you order — the more you buy, the lower the price per piece. In the Design Studio you see your total before checkout; for a quote we price it for you.
How fast can you get my order done?
Most orders take 7–14 business days, depending on the project. Faster turnaround is available on request. Have a deadline? Tell us the date. Rush fees may apply.
How do I get a quote?
Tell us the item, quantity, and your idea — through the form below, live chat, or a quick text — and we'll price it. Or design it yourself instantly in our Design Studio.
I don't have a design — can you help?
Yes — free design help is included. If your image is low-resolution or you have a complex idea, a real person will make it print-ready. You can also build it yourself in our online Design Studio with uploads, fonts, graphics, and a built-in AI designer.
What file types do you need?
Designing online: upload PNG, JPG, or WEBP, 300 DPI or higher. Getting a quote: we take vector files (AI, EPS, SVG), high-res PNG, PSD, embroidery files (DST, PES, EXP, JEF, VP3, XXX, EMB) and ZIPs. Not sure? Send it and we'll check.
Can I order for a group or business?
Yes — family reunions, teams, events, churches, and business uniforms are our specialty. Order any quantity, from one to bulk.
Where are you located, and can I pick up?
We're in Chicago, with curbside pickup at 3047 N Lincoln Ave #435, Mon–Fri 10:30 AM–6 PM. Call or text (773) 849-1854 when you arrive.
Do you ship?
Yes — we ship nationwide. Local Chicago customers can also use curbside pickup. Shipping cost is included in your quote.
How do I care for my custom apparel?
Machine wash cold, inside-out, and tumble dry low. Skip bleach and high heat, and your print will last for years.

Still have a question? Send us a message or text (773) 849-1854.
