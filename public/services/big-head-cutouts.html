How big is big?

Every size below is drawn to the same scale, so you can see what you are choosing before you order. The measurement is the height of the finished cutout.

A pack is a sheet, or half of one

Cutouts are printed on a sheet and cut out of it, so a sheet is the natural unit. The price of a pack is the same whether you order one or ten, and delivery is already in it — which makes it the cheapest way to buy at every size.

Questions

Big head cutouts, answered

What is a big head cutout?
A photo of a face, printed on rigid board and cut around the outline, so it stands up on its own or gets held up at the event. People bring them to graduations, birthdays, weddings, send-offs and games.
What photo do you need from me?
The clearest one you have — a straight-on shot of the face, as large in the frame as possible. Phone photos are fine. Send it by text or email and we will tell you before we print if it is not going to hold up at the size you want.
Why is the 36 inch sold only by the sheet?
A 36 inch head is wider than the board a single is cut from, so there is no smaller way to make one. It comes 5 to a sheet and that sheet is the smallest order.
Can I order just one 24 inch?
The 24 inch is sold 2 or more at a time, at $40 each. Each one is printed, mounted and cut out by hand.
Can I mix sizes in one order?
Yes. Each size is quoted on its own line, and the quantity across the order is what sets the price on each one.
Can every cutout be a different face?
Yes — a pack does not have to be one photo repeated. Send one photo per cutout and say how many of each.
How long do they take?
They are printed to order. Ask when you send the photo and we will give you a date before you pay — we do not quote a turnaround we cannot hold.
