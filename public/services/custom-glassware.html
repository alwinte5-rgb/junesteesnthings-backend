The service

Personalized glasses for every occasion

Custom glassware turns a toast into a keepsake. We personalize wine glasses, stemless glasses, champagne flutes, shot glasses, beer glasses and rocks glasses with names, dates, monograms and short messages, in vinyl or etched finishes. Bridal parties, birthdays, anniversaries, Valentine's Day and corporate gifts are our most common glassware orders.

Engraved tumblers and flasks

Stainless steel tumblers with an engraved monogram or name keep drinks cold and last for years, and engraved flasks make classic groomsmen gifts. We also make full-color photo tumblers with sublimation for something more personal.

Party glassware and favors

Shot glasses with the birthday year, "Bride Squad" wine glasses, custom cups for a milestone party, and etched glasses as client or partner gifts make favors people take home. Order a set for everyone, each with their own name.

Gift sets

We put glasses together with wine bags, gift boxes, roses and signs to make ready-to-give sets for Valentine's Day, Mother's Day, weddings and retirements. Tell us the budget and the person, and we will suggest a set.

Glassware for birthdays and milestones

Milestone birthdays (21st, 30th, 40th, 50th) are perfect for shot glasses and wine glasses with the year and the guest of honor's name, or a different name on each glass for the whole party. Pair them with matching shirts and a banner for a complete celebration.

Corporate and client gifts

Etched glasses, engraved tumblers and branded drinkware make lasting client and employee gifts, especially for the holidays, closings and anniversaries. We add your logo, a short message and each recipient's name, and pack every gift ready to hand out.

Caring for your custom glassware

Vinyl lettering lasts longest when glasses are washed by hand and not soaked; etched and engraved designs are permanent and dishwasher safe on most glass. We include care notes with every order.

Wedding and bridal party glassware

Weddings are where glassware shines: Mr and Mrs champagne flutes for the toast, wine glasses with each bridesmaid's name, "Bride Squad" tumblers for the bachelorette trip, engraved flasks for the groomsmen, and etched glasses with the date as guest favors. We match your wedding colors and fonts and can make the welcome sign and table signs to go with them, so the whole day looks like one set.

Engraving, etching and vinyl: what is the difference?

Engraving cuts the design into stainless steel or coated tumblers and flasks, so it never wears off. Etching frosts the surface of the glass for an elegant, permanent look that is dishwasher safe on most glass. Vinyl lettering comes in many colors and fonts, including glitter and metallic, and is ideal for party glasses and gifts that will be washed by hand. We will recommend the right finish for how the glasses will be used.

Glassware for the holidays

Personalized glassware is one of our most requested holiday gifts: Christmas wine glasses and mugs with each family member's name, Valentine's champagne flutes for couples, Mother's Day and Father's Day tumblers, and New Year's Eve flutes for a party. We can match a set to your theme colors and wrap it as a gift set, ready to hand over.

How to order custom glassware

Glassware is quoted rather than designed online. Send us the names or wording, the type of glass, the quantity and your date, and we reply with a proof and a price. Our standard turnaround is 7 to 14 business days after proof approval; pick up at our Lakeview shop by appointment, or we ship carefully packed.

Yes, every glass in a set can carry its own name.
Is the lettering dishwasher safe?
Hand washing is best for vinyl lettering. Etched and engraved finishes are permanent.
Do you engrave stainless steel tumblers?
Yes, names, initials and monograms.

There is no minimum on most items; sets of the same design cost less per piece.

Yes, with glasses, a wine bag or box, and a sign or card.
