Questions businesses ask

What is the June's Tees Business Partners Program?

A free program for Chicago businesses and organizations that order custom apparel, embroidery, signs or printing. Partners get 15% off their first order of $150 or more, 5% off every reorder, a 60-day price lock on repeat jobs, and one account for logos, proofs, order status, team sizes and reorders.

Who can join?

Any business or organization, of any size: one-person shops, freelancers, salons and barbershops, restaurants, contractors, trucking companies, cleaning services, real estate teams, nonprofits, churches, schools, sports teams, unions and large companies.

Does it cost anything, or do I need documents?

No. Joining is free, with no membership fee and no minimum order. You only need an email address and your business name. We never ask for an EIN, a license or an ID to join.

How does the 15% off work?

Your first business order of $150 or more (before tax and delivery) gets 15% off . When you are signed in, or when we send a quote to an email on your business, the discount is applied for you. It can be used once per business and does not combine with another discount.

What do I save when I reorder?

Every reorder after a paid order gets 5% off. If you reorder the same items in the same quantity range within 60 days of paying, you also keep the price you paid.

Is there a minimum order for custom business shirts?

No minimums. DTF printing makes a few shirts affordable, screen printing is the best value for larger runs, and embroidery gives polos, jackets and hats a premium look. We recommend the right method for your logo and quantity.

How long does a business order take?

Most orders take 7 to 14 business days, depending on the job. Have a deadline, like an opening, an event or a new hire start date? Tell us the date when you ask for a quote. Rush fees may apply.

Can my staff order their own sizes?

Yes. Make a size link from your quote and share it. Each person types their name and how many of each size they need, youth sizes included, and the totals go on your quote.

We are tax-exempt. Can we still join?

Yes. Nonprofits, churches and schools can join and upload an Illinois tax-exemption certificate on the quote. An approved certificate carries over to your reorders.

Do you deliver, or can I pick up?

Free pickup at our Lincoln Avenue shop in Lakeview, by appointment, plus shipping and local delivery. We serve all of Chicago and the suburbs.

The details

  • 15% off your first order: the items on one order of $150 or more (before tax and delivery). One use per business, and not with another discount on the same order.
  • 5% off reorders: when you order again after a paid order with us. One discount per order.
  • Discounts go on when you're signed in to your account, or when we send a quote to an email on your business. Typing a different code replaces it.
  • 60-day price: reorder the same items, in the same quantity range, within 60 days of paying and you keep the price you paid (before any discount). A change of product, color, artwork or quantity range gets a fresh quote.
  • Orders start with a 50% deposit; orders under $100 are paid in full. You approve a proof before anything is printed.
  • Shopping for yourself? Nothing changes. You can still order the way you always have.
