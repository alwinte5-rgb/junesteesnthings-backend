## Feedback on your concept and logo

The hero concept is the right direction: a warm family photo, a big headline and our logo at the top. Five changes before it goes out:

1. **Shirts we can really print.** The designs sit on cable-knit sweaters, which we cannot print on. Use crewneck sweatshirts, long sleeves or tees.
2. **Their name, not ours.** Show the product people buy: "Christmas with the Johnsons 2026" (a made-up family name) in your lettering.
3. **Every print visible.** Pose or crop so no print is hidden behind a child or an arm.
4. **A licensed photo.** A stock photo we have a license for, or our own customers with written permission. Keep the license in the folder.
5. **Fix the tagline.** We don't sell holiday cards. Use "Matching family shirts, photo gifts and holiday party gear. Made in Lakeview, Chicago."

The "Christmas at June's Tees" logo: keep the idea, with these changes.

- **Make it the product, too.** Build a fill-in version: "Christmas at the ______'s" with the year. Keep "Christmas at June's Tees" as our seasonal badge (stickers, packaging, the corner of posts).
- **Tie it to the brand.** Add hot pink #E0107A on the ribbon or the hat trim.
- **Dark-shirt version.** Add a white or cream outline around the red letters.
- **Bolder gift box.** Fill it with solid color and thicken the lines.
- **The apostrophe.** Move the hat so "June's" shows its apostrophe.
